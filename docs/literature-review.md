# Literature review

Accessed 15 November 2026. This is a targeted literature review, not an exhaustive novelty search. Metadata was checked against the linked primary publication pages; method summaries use abstracts and available paper text. A limitation below can be a mismatch with our task rather than a flaw in the cited work. No published performance numbers are reused as our results.

| **Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts** — Nelson F. We do not evaluate the generator or claim to solve position bias. |
| **Provence: efficient and robust context pruning for retrieval-augmented generation** — Nadezhda Chirkova, Thibault Formal, Vassilina Nikoulina, Stéphane Clinchant (2025) | [arXiv:2501.16214](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16214) | Integrate sentence-level context pruning with reranking. | Strong learned pruning is an important future comparator. | **Submodular Maximization with Uncertain Knapsack Capacity** — Yasushi Kawase, Hanna Sumita, Takuro Fukunaga (2019; preprint 2018) | [DOI:10.1137/18M1174428](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/abs/10.1137/18M1174428), [arXiv:1803.02565](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02565) | Adaptive and precomputed universal policies for uncertain capacity. | Direct theoretical prior art: universal/incremental selection is not new. Single-paragraph answers do not establish multi-hop support or actual answer correctness. |

## What was learned

1. Retrieval, compression, submodular selection and universal capacity policies all predate this project.
2. A useful engineering question is whether a finite, **jointly planned budget ladder**, charged for unique source words and accompanied by a recomputable source manifest, improves the stability/coverage tradeoff over independently rebuilt packs.
3. Evaluate source retention separately from generation; do not relabel a retrieval proxy as QA accuracy.
4. Exact enumeration/DP on small pools can expose search failures. A good proxy optimum can still discard the answer when retrieval scores are misleading.
5. Learned pruning, dense retrieval, multilingual text, multi-hop evidence and actual model-token budgets remain necessary follow-up evaluations.
